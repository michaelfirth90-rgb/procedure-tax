Four corrections to what I issued before the legislation arrived
ItemCorrection
Finding 3I said VATA 1994 ss.59–59B were "omitted by FA 2021, Sch 27". Imprecise. They are omitted by Sch 27 para 17, and only for specified purposes on three separate dates — 1 January 2023, 6 April 2024 and 1 April 2026 — which is why they still appear in the consolidated text. The commencing instruments are SI 2022/1278 reg 2(3)(4)(a), SI 2024/440 reg 2 and SI 2026/370 reg 3.
Finding 5My replacement read "fourteen years" in words, because the site had "seven years" in words. FA 2003 s.95(2)(b) as amended uses the numeral — 14 years.
Finding 7I "corrected" FA 2004 s.72A(3) from "final appeal" to "further appeal", assuming a conversion slip. The statute genuinely reads "any appeal or final appeal". My correction was the error. Likewise s.62A(3) opens "a 'connected party' is" with no dash — that is the statute, not an artefact.
Legislation listItem 15, the 2026 appointed-day instrument I could not identify, is SI 2026/370. It can come off the list.
Rule 35, the CIS provisions, ITEPA ss.61Y and 61Z, the VAT thresholds and FA 2026 s.265 all verified word for word against the Acts. Two commas were missing from my transcription of r.35(2) and s.66(7)(b); both are right below.
